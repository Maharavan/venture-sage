Analyze the risks associated with the company, market, product, and execution strategy.

1. Technology Risks

   * Infrastructure dependencies
   * Security vulnerabilities

   * Reliability risks

2. Market Risks

   * Market saturation
   * Weak demand signals
   * Competitive pressures
   * Customer concentration
   * Market timing concerns

3. Regulatory Risks

   * Industry regulations
   * Compliance requirements
   * Data privacy concerns
   * Government policy changes
   * Geographic restrictions

4. Operational Risks

   * Hiring challenges
   * Talent dependency
   * Key-person risk
   * Organizational scaling challenges
   * Operational inefficiencies

5. Execution Risks

   * Founder execution capability
   * Product-market fit uncertainty
   * Go-to-market challenges
   * Revenue execution concerns
   * Fundraising dependency

For each risk:

* Assess likelihood
* Assess potential impact
* Explain supporting evidence

Identify:

* Top risks
* Potential mitigations
* Areas requiring monitoring

Assign a risk score using:

0.0-2.9   Low Risk
3.0-4.9   Moderate Risk
5.0-6.9   Elevated Risk
7.0-8.9   High Risk
9.0-10.0 Critical Risk

Important:

* Higher score means higher risk.
* Do not invent risks without evidence.
* If information is unavailable, state "Not Found".

Populate `technology_risks` (minimum 2 items) — specific technology risks identified.

Populate `market_risks` (minimum 2 items) — specific market-level risks identified.

Populate `regulatory_risks` (minimum 1 item) — specific regulatory or compliance risks; write ["None identified"] only if truly none exist.

Populate `operational_risks` (minimum 2 items) — specific operational risks identified.

Populate `execution_risks` (minimum 2 items) — specific execution risks identified.

Populate `risk_mitigations` (minimum 2 items) — specific mitigations or safeguards that reduce the risks above.

For `recommendation` use EXACTLY one of these values: Proceed | Proceed with Caution | Caution Advised | High Caution | Avoid

Provide:

* Technology risks
* Market risks
* Regulatory risks
* Operational risks
* Execution risks
* Risk mitigations
* Key risks
* Risk score
* Score rationale
* Recommendation
* Summary

Summary:

- Technology, market, regulatory, operational, and execution risks found
